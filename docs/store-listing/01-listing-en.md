**Full description:**

```
Sitting at a desk all day? Posture Reminder nudges you to sit up straight, stretch and rest your eyes at the interval you choose, so back, neck and shoulder tension never builds up.

Set a reminder every 15, 30 or 60 minutes (or any custom number of minutes), tap START, and get on with your work. The app does the remembering for you.

WHAT YOU GET

* Posture reminders: gentle notifications that tell you exactly what to do: straighten your back, pull your shoulders back, stretch your neck, squeeze your shoulder blades.
* Eye break reminder (20-20-20 rule): every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. A simple habit that helps against digital eye strain from screens.
* Stand up and stretch: 5 short desk stretches (shoulder shrugs, neck stretch, shoulder blade squeeze, wrist stretch, torso twist) that take only a couple of minutes.
* Posture-strengthening exercises: 5 equipment-free exercises (wall push-up, chair squat, plank, calf raise, seated core squeeze) you can do at the office or at home.
* Guided breathing: 4-7-8 breathing, box breathing and coherent breathing with an animated guide. Perfect for a stress break, focus, or winding down before sleep.
* Streaks and statistics: track your daily streak, total sessions, the last 7 days and a breakdown by type. Unlock milestones at 7, 30 and 100 days.
* Share your progress: send the app or your streak to a friend or colleague.

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE

* Alert mode: silent, vibrate or sound, with light, medium or strong vibration.
* Night quiet hours: no reminders while you sleep.
* Snooze or mark Done straight from the notification.
* Personalised with your name, and a bank of varied reminder messages so they never feel repetitive.
* Light, dark or system theme.
* Available in English and Turkish.
* Your name and preferences stay on your device.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Office workers, remote workers, students, gamers, programmers, designers, drivers and anyone who spends hours in front of a computer or phone. If you have ever thought "I should really sit up straight" or "my eyes are tired", this is for you.

WHY SMALL BREAKS WORK

Long periods of static sitting are tough on your neck, shoulders and lower back. Short, regular movement breaks and looking away from the screen are simple, widely recommended habits for a more comfortable workday. Posture Reminder makes those habits automatic, without complicated setup or accounts.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Choose your interval with the dial or a preset.
2. Turn on the eye break reminder if you like.
3. Tap START.
4. When a reminder arrives, straighten up, stretch, or open a short session.
5. Build your streak day by day.

Download Posture Reminder and make good posture and healthy screen habits effortless.

Note: This app is a wellness reminder tool and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you have pain or a health concern, consult a professional.
```
