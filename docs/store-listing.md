# Google Play Store Listing — Duruş Hatırlatıcı (DeskFit)

Store copy and ASO research for Google Play. Every feature claim below maps to
something that ships in the app: posture reminders (interval, quiet hours,
snooze/done actions, sound/vibration), 20-20-20 eye-rest reminders, posture
check-ins with a daily goal ring + streak, Focus/Pomodoro tab (25/5, 50/10,
90/15) with break movement suggestions, Meditation tab (desk stretches,
no-equipment posture exercises, eye exercises, 4 breathing techniques), stats
with a 7-day chart, badges, a visual share card, dark mode, TR/EN UI.

Rules we follow (Google Play metadata policy):

- No medical/health claims ("cures back pain", "fixes posture"). We say the app
  *reminds*, *helps you build a habit*, *guides* — never that it treats anything.
- No keyword stuffing, no ALL CAPS, no emoji in the title, no "#1"/"best"/"free"
  ranking or price claims, no references to other apps.
- Character counts are Unicode characters as Play Console counts them.
- ES / DE / PT-BR listings should only be published once those UI locales ship
  (being added separately). Until then, a listing in a language the app does not
  speak will cause bad reviews.

## ⭐ Ratings

The in-app review prompt (`lib/review.js`) is shown at most once, at a
"happy moment": after the 5th completed session, or after the user dismisses
the "daily goal reached" alert — but only once the goal has been met on at
least 2 different days (so never on the first day of use).
